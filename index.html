Privacy Policy

MathMitra — Mathematics Learning App

Effective Date: June 27, 2026

Welcome to MathMitra

Thank you for choosing MathMitra.

Your privacy matters to us. MathMitra has been designed with a privacy-first philosophy, ensuring that your learning experience remains secure and under your control.

Unlike many modern applications, MathMitra does not require you to create an account, sign in, or share your personal information to use its features.

Our Privacy Commitment

MathMitra is built around a simple principle:

Your learning data belongs to you.

All your educational data—including notes, bookmarks, revision packs, learning history, preferences, and settings—is stored only on your device.

MathMitra does not upload, synchronize, or store your personal learning data on our servers.

Information We Collect

MathMitra itself does not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Date of birth
  • Passwords
  • Payment information

You can use MathMitra without creating an account or providing any personal details.

Local Data Storage

To provide a personalized learning experience, MathMitra stores certain information locally on your device, including:

  • Bookmarks
  • Notes
  • Revision Packs
  • Practice History
  • Saved Formula Collections
  • App Preferences
  • Learning Progress

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to export it using features provided by the application.

Camera Permission

MathMitra requests access to your device's camera only when you use the Scan OCR feature.

The camera is used exclusively to capture mathematical expressions for recognition and solving.

OCR processing is performed locally within the application. Images captured for OCR are not uploaded to our servers.

Internet Connection Required

MathMitra is a free application supported by advertisements.

An active internet connection is required to load advertisements through Google AdMob, helping support the continued development and maintenance of the application.

Apart from serving advertisements, MathMitra does not use the internet connection to upload, synchronize, or store your personal learning data.

Your learning data always remains on your device.

Advertising

MathMitra displays advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect certain device and advertising-related information in accordance with Google's own Privacy Policy to provide and measure advertisements.

MathMitra itself does not collect, store, or share your personal learning data with advertisers.

Data Security

Since your learning information remains on your own device, you maintain control over your data.

We recommend protecting your device using appropriate security measures such as screen locks, biometric authentication, and regular software updates.

Children's Privacy

MathMitra is an educational application intended for students, teachers, and lifelong learners.

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect improvements, new features, or legal requirements.

The latest version will always be available within the application.
